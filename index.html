Board-answer formula

AnswerGive the direct answer first.
ExplainGive the reason or standard.
ApplySay what you would do.

Board members may ask two situational or fact-based questions from each subject area. Speak each answer aloud in 20–40 seconds. Never bluff a regulation number or medical detail: give the best accurate answer you know, then say you would verify the current publication or SOP.
