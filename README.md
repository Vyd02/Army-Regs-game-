# 261st MMB Board Prep

A study app for the 261st Multifunctional Medical Battalion October 2026 Soldier / NCO / Officer of the Month board, built from the board study guide plus common board questions (tagged "Added").

Open `index.html` in any browser (no install or build step). It works offline once the fonts have loaded.

- **HQ**: rank and XP (Private to Sergeant Major of the Army), daily streak, 12 medals, today's mission, and readiness by subject.
- **Study**: notes and practice questions for all 17 subject areas, the self-check list, and official reference links.
- **Flashcards**: every practice question plus the publication numbers and acronyms; cards you miss come back.
- **Quiz**: 10 questions, Rapid Fire (60 seconds), or Sudden Death, with combo XP multipliers.
- **Mock Board**: two random questions per subject area with a timer and self-grading (Soldier, NCO, or Officer board).
- **Drills**: back azimuth trainer, nine-line MEDEVAC ordering, acronym recall.

Progress is saved in the browser. This is a study aid; verify everything against current Army publications and your unit SOP.
